How AI was used
---------------
I asked Codex to review my initial grammar draft and the Stage 1 assignment,
then begin implementing the project while respecting the grammar, tests,
implementation restrictions, and commit requirements. Codex inspected the
repository and modified or created:

- grammar.ebnf: clarified whitespace, newline, UTF-8, identifier, reserved-word,
  source-position, and Stage 2 semantic-check conventions;
- lexer.py: hand-written byte scanner, positioned tokens, UTF-8 kaomoji
  recognition, and lexical diagnostics;
- ast_nodes.py: AST class hierarchy and stable indented dump;
- parser.py: hand-written recursive-descent parser with peek/eat;
- compiler.py: the --ast CLI and required error/output behavior;
- tests/: valid and invalid golden cases plus a test runner;
- README.md: language description, examples, usage, and test instructions;
- ai_usage.txt: this disclosure.
